How the 9-Month Pathway Works

N4 · Months 1–3

Foundation knowledge, terminology, basic concepts, introductory practical activities and first assessments.

N5 · Months 4–6

Intermediate concepts, applied learning, projects, problem solving and deeper assessments.

N6 · Months 7–9

Advanced learning, professional practice, capstone project, portfolio preparation and final assessment.

Important: LearnHub's N4, N5 and N6 labels describe its internal learning pathway. They should not be presented as an officially accredited South African N4/N5/N6 qualification unless the appropriate accreditation has been obtained.
