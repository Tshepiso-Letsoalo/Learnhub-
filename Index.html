🎤 Interview Preparation

Practise common interview questions.

1. Tell us about yourself.

Introduce yourself, your experience, skills and career goals.

2. Why should we hire you?

Explain your relevant skills, willingness to learn and contribution.

3. What are your strengths?

Give genuine strengths and examples.

4. Where do you see yourself in five years?

Explain your career goals and skills you want to develop.

5. Tell us about a challenge you faced.

Explain the situation, what you did and what you learned.
